Astraum - credits and third-party notices
==============================================

This file lists the data Astraum contains and the licences of the
software that is part of Astraum.exe. Keep it with the executable
when you pass the program on.

==============================================================================
PART 1 - DATA AND PICTURES
==============================================================================
Astraum contains the following data. The pictures were reduced and
reprojected for the game (tools/make_maps.py, make_detail_maps.py,
make_body_maps.py, make_sky.py); assets/maps/SOURCES.txt and
assets/sky/SOURCES.txt list the exact products.

Earth
  NASA Blue Marble (land and oceans), NASA Earth Observatory / NASA Goddard
  Space Flight Center. Elevation: NASA Blue Marble SRTM elevation ramp.
Moon
  NASA Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon
  Kit (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720): LRO LROC WAC colour and LRO LOLA
  elevation. NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University.
Mars
  Viking MDIM 2.1 colour mosaic, USGS Astrogeology Science Center / NASA Ames.
  Elevation: MGS MOLA MEGDR, NASA PDS Geosciences Node.
Mercury
  MESSENGER MDIS global mosaic, NASA/Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics
  Laboratory/Carnegie Institution of Washington, via USGS Astrogeology.
Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto
  Voyager and Galileo SSI global mosaics, NASA/JPL, via USGS Astrogeology.
Enceladus
  Cassini ISS global mosaic, NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute,
  via USGS Astrogeology.
Triton
  Voyager 2 colour mosaic, NASA/JPL, via USGS Astrogeology.
Pluto, Charon
  New Horizons LORRI/MVIC global mosaics, NASA/Johns Hopkins University
  Applied Physics Laboratory/Southwest Research Institute, via USGS
  Astrogeology.
Ceres
  Dawn Framing Camera global mosaic, NASA/JPL-Caltech/UCLA/MPS/DLR/IDA,
  produced by DLR, via USGS Astrogeology.
Phobos
  Mars Express SRC global mosaic (26 SRC and 8 Viking images), German
  Aerospace Center (DLR), Institute of Planetary Research; ESA/DLR/FU Berlin.
  Authors: M. Wählisch et al. (DLR). Via USGS Astrogeology, where it is
  listed as public domain with the request to cite the authors.
Jupiter
  Cassini's best maps of Jupiter (PIA07782), NASA/JPL/Space Science
  Institute.
Stars
  Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th revised edition, D. Hoffleit and
  W. H. Warren Jr. (1991), distributed by the Harvard-Smithsonian Center for
  Astrophysics.
Milky Way
  NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, "Deep
  Star Maps 2020" (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851).
  Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC.
Comets
  Orbital elements from the JPL Small-Body Database, NASA/JPL-Caltech.

The use of these pictures does not imply any endorsement of Astraum by
NASA, ESA, DLR, the USGS or any other agency or institution.

Surface base buildings: modelled in Astraum's own code after published
concepts (3D-printed regolith habitats, inflatable habitats, the EDEN ISS
greenhouse, ISRU plants, NASA Fission Surface Power, VSAT solar towers);
their textures are generated by the program. No third-party models or
textures are used.

Fonts: Astraum draws its text with the fonts installed in Windows and
does not contain or distribute any font.
