Astraum surface maps - sources
====================================

All maps below are public domain (works of NASA / USGS). They were reduced to the game's size and
layout with tools/make_maps.py (equirectangular, column 0 = 180 W, row 0 = 90 N; elevation stored
losslessly as 16 bit codes: metres = code * 0.6 - 12000). earth_color.jpg and moon_color.jpg are written
by tools/make_detail_maps.py from the same pictures as their high-resolution tiles (both levels match).

earth_color.jpg   NASA Blue Marble "Whole world - land and oceans 12000" (24000 x 12000), reduced
                  (via Wikimedia Commons: File:Whole_world_-_land_and_oceans_12000.jpg)
earth_height.png  NASA Blue Marble SRTM elevation ramp "Srtm_ramp2.world.21600x10800.jpg"
                  (via Wikimedia Commons); grey 12 = sea level, calibrated on Everest (8,848 m)
moon_color.jpg    NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (LRO LROC WAC colour with poles, 8k), reduced
                  https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
moon_height.png   NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit, LRO LOLA elevation "ldem_4" (4 px/degree)
mars_color.jpg    USGS Astrogeology / NASA Ames, Viking MDIM 2.1 colour mosaic (1 km)
                  (via Wikimedia Commons: File:Mars_Viking_MDIM21_ClrMosaic_1km.jpg)
mars_height.png   NASA PDS Geosciences Node, MGS MOLA MEGDR "megt90n000cb" (4 px/degree)
                  https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/mgs/mgs-m-mola-5-megdr-l3-v1/

The relief of the other solid bodies is generated by the game (craters and hills), not taken from data.

High-resolution tiles (tools/make_detail_maps.py; 16 x 8 tiles each, shown near the body):
earth_color_hi.pak   the Blue Marble picture above at 8192 x 4096
earth_height_hi.pak  the SRTM elevation ramp above at 8192 x 4096
moon_color_hi.pak    NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit, LROC WAC colour with poles 8k (8192 x 4096)
                     https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
moon_height_hi.pak   NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit, LRO LOLA elevation "ldem_16" (16 px/degree, 5760 x 2880)
mars_color_hi.pak    the Viking MDIM 2.1 colour mosaic above at 8192 x 4096
mars_height_hi.pak   NASA PDS Geosciences Node, MGS MOLA MEGDR "megt90n000eb" (16 px/degree, 5760 x 2880)
                     https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/mgs/mgs-m-mola-5-megdr-l3-v1/

Colour maps of the bodies whose relief is generated (tools/make_body_maps.py; 4096 x 2048, Mercury 8192 x 4096;
gaps without data filled with the mosaic's mean colour; black-and-white mosaics tinted with the body's colour).
All from the USGS Astrogeology Science Center map archive (https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/mosaic/), listed there as
public domain (Phobos: Mars Express SRC mosaic by DLR, ESA/DLR/FU Berlin; USGS asks to cite the authors, M. Waehlisch et al.),
except Jupiter (NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute, NASA Photojournal PIA07782):
mercury_color.jpg    MESSENGER MDIS basemap, MD3 colour mosaic, global 64 px/deg (NASA/JHUAPL/CIW), desaturated
io_color.jpg         Io Galileo SSI / Voyager global mosaic, colour merge, 1 km
europa_color.jpg     Europa Voyager / Galileo SSI global mosaic, 500 m
ganymede_color.jpg   Ganymede Voyager / Galileo SSI global colour mosaic, 1.4 km
callisto_color.jpg   Callisto Voyager / Galileo SSI global mosaic, 1 km
enceladus_color.jpg  Enceladus Cassini ISS global mosaic, 110 m
triton_color.jpg     Triton Voyager 2 colour mosaic (global fill), 600 m
pluto_color.jpg      Pluto New Horizons LORRI / MVIC global mosaic, 300 m (July 2017)
charon_color.jpg     Charon New Horizons LORRI / MVIC global mosaic, 300 m (July 2017)
ceres_color.jpg      Ceres Dawn FC global mosaic (DLR), 20 px/deg (October 2015)
phobos_color.jpg     Phobos Mars Express SRC global mosaic, 16 px/deg
jupiter_color.jpg    Cassini's best maps of Jupiter, cylindrical map (December 2000), PIA07782

No colour map: Venus and Titan (their clouds and haze are what is seen), Saturn, Uranus and Neptune (no spacecraft
map of the whole planet in the public domain), Deimos and Titania (no global mosaic); these stay procedural.
