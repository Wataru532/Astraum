Astraum night sky - sources
================================

Built by tools/make_sky.py.

stars.bin     Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th revised edition (Hoffleit & Warren, 1991): the 9,096 stars with a
              J2000 position and a V magnitude (the naked-eye sky, to about 6.5 mag), with their B-V colours.
              ASCII edition distributed by the Harvard-Smithsonian Center for Astrophysics:
              http://tdc-www.harvard.edu/catalogs/bsc5.html

milkyway.jpg  NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, "Deep Star Maps 2020"
              (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851), the Milky Way background "milkyway_2020_8k.exr".
              Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio. Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC.
              Equirectangular in equatorial J2000 coordinates (RA 12h at the left edge, RA increasing to the left),
              tone-mapped to 8 bits.
